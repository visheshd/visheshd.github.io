The Case Against startup hiring event (And a Smarter Alternative)

June 19, 2024
startup hiring

A founder friend of mine pulled off one of the smartest hiring moves I've seen this year:

He hosted a happy hour for engineers — and asked a few trusted devs in his network to bring +1s.

No booths. No name tags. No careers page.

Just real people, real conversations… and high-context signal.

He hired two top-notch engineers from that one evening.

No recruiters. No cold sourcing. No $10K/month hiring software.

Meanwhile, founders are burning time and cash on startup hiring events that feel like speed dating — with worse matches.

Let's talk about why traditional hiring events are broken — and what actually works.

Why Most Startup Hiring Events Fail

1. They're built for optics, not outcomes

You show up. You pitch the company. You collect resumes.
But you leave with surface-level connections and zero confidence in next steps.

2. They attract generalists, not builders

Hiring events bring in the curious, not the committed.
People interested in "startups" — not necessarily your mission or your mess.

3. They skip the filter that matters most: referral signal

Your best hires will come through people you trust.
Not through a cold badge scan at a hiring fair.

A Smarter Alternative: Founder-Led, Peer-Referred, Low-Pressure Events

That happy hour worked because it had:

✅ A clear target: engineers
✅ Peer-to-peer trust baked in (via invite-only referrals)
✅ Zero pressure, high-signal conversation
✅ A founder who knew what he was looking for

It wasn't just a hiring event.
It was a fit filter disguised as a friendly drink.

Other Smart Moves You Can Steal

🛠️ Start with a scoped build

Give someone a real task before you give them a contract.
See how they think. How they handle ambiguity. How they communicate.

💬 Use content to attract like-minded builders

Share how you work. Be honest about what's broken.
You don't need to "sell" your startup — just tell the truth.

The right people lean in. The wrong ones filter out.

📩 Auto-reply filters > job boards

Ask for a short async task before even scheduling an interview.
9 out of 10 won't reply. That's your filter.

TL;DR

Startup hiring events sound smart — but often deliver noise.

If you want real hires, skip the stage. Skip the swag.
Create spaces where signal can emerge naturally — and use your existing network to filter for fit.
