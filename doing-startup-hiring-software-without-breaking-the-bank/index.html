Doing startup hiring software Without Breaking the Bank

June 17, 2024
startup hiring

Hiring software is supposed to help you scale.

But most early-stage founders end up paying for complexity they don't need — and ignoring simple tactics that actually work.

You don't need enterprise-level ATS tools.
You need high-signal, low-friction ways to find the one person who can actually help right now.

Here's how to do startup hiring software — without breaking the bank.

Step 1: Don't Buy Tools Before You Have a Problem

Most founders sign up for hiring platforms before they know:

  • What the role actually is
  • What success looks like
  • Whether the work even requires a full-time hire

You don't need a subscription to post vague job descriptions.

You need clarity and a way to filter for high-agency candidates.

Step 2: Use This One Hack to Filter Instantly

Here's a simple move that outperforms most hiring software:

Set up an auto-reply asking for one specific task.

Example:

"Thanks for applying! Could you send 3 bullet points on how you'd approach [key task]?"

Or:

"Could you record a 2-minute Loom explaining your favorite past project?"

What happens?
✅ 90% of candidates won't reply — and that's a good thing.
✅ The 10% who do are serious, self-motivated, and worth your time.

No ATS needed. No spreadsheets. Just signal.

Step 3: Stack Smart (If You Still Need Tools)

If you're getting more traction and need light structure, try this lean tool stack:

  • Notion / Airtable / Trello — DIY tracking
  • Ashby Lite / Homerun / Recruitee — affordable, founder-friendly ATS
  • Typeform + Email filters — build your own pre-screening flow

But don't go tool-hunting until you're actually hiring.

Step 4: Build Internal Tools Before You Build a Team

Before hiring ops, growth, or support:

  • Ask: Can this be automated or delegated to AI?
  • Build: Use GPT + Sheets, Zapier, and basic APIs
  • Test: Prove the problem is real before hiring a human

That's how you keep your burn low and your output high.

TL;DR

Most startup hiring software is overkill.
What works is:

  • Clarity on what needs to be done
  • Smart pre-screening (like the auto-reply test)
  • Lean internal tools
  • High-agency people, not high-volume applicants
