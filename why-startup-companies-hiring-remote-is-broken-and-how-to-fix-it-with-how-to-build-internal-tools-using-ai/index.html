Why startup companies hiring remote Is Broken (And How To Fix It With how to build internal tools using AI)

June 10, 2024
startup hiring

Remote hiring isn't broken because remote doesn't work.

It's broken because most startups hire the wrong way.

They look at the wrong signals:

  • Pedigree
  • Big company logos
  • Number of years in a similar role

But when you're hiring remotely — especially early stage — none of that matters if one thing is missing:

High agency.

What Is High Agency?

It's the ability to:

  • Figure things out without waiting for instructions
  • Navigate ambiguity with confidence
  • See a messy problem, and own the solution
  • Communicate clearly, deliver fast, and course-correct without drama

You can't see it on a resume.
You notice it only after someone's already shipping.

And by then, it's too late.

Why Most Remote Hiring Misses the Mark

Startups hire based on:

  • What someone has done
  • Where they've worked
  • Whether they check the "remote-ready" boxes

But that doesn't guarantee they'll ship without handholding, or thrive in your async chaos.

What you really need is someone who acts like a builder, not a joiner.

The Alternative: Hire for Outcomes, Not Optics

Before defaulting to remote hiring, ask:

  • Can I turn this into a tool instead of a team?
  • Is the task repeatable enough to automate?
  • Can I test for agency before hiring?

You'll often find you don't need to hire — you need to build.

How Internal Tools Using AI Can Help

Instead of hiring hoping for high-agency talent, build internal tools that:

✅ Handle the repetitive
✅ Make outcomes visible
✅ Create leverage without bloating headcount

Examples:

  • Lead Scoring → Use GPT + LinkedIn + scoring dashboard
  • Growth Experiment Tracker → Use Notion + GPT auto-analysis
  • Support Triage → Use Intercom + GPT for auto-replies & handoffs

These tools don't need onboarding. They don't ghost you. And they work.

TL;DR

Remote hiring is broken not because of location — but because we look at the wrong signals.

What you need is high-agency execution.
And in most cases, you can unlock that by:

  • Building smart internal tools
  • Working with high-agency builders (like former founders)
  • Only hiring after you've proven the need
